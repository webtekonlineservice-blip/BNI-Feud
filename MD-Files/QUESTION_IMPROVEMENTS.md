# BNI Feud - Question Improvements

## Summary
Successfully researched and created **24 ORIGINAL, VARIED Family Feud questions** for all 12 chapter members.

## What Changed

### Original Problems
- Questions were **too predictable** and one-dimensional
- Focused only on each member's profession in a stereotypical way
- **Every question started with "Name..."** - boring and repetitive
- Answers were obvious and not engaging
- Example: "Name something Fredrick would do if he saw a risk not in his spreadsheet"

### After Improvements
- ✨ **NO MORE "Name..." repetition** - varied question formats
- **More diverse topics** - mix of professional, personal, and universal scenarios
- **More relatable** - situations everyone has experienced
- **Better humor** - subtle and clever instead of forced
- **Unexpected answers** - logical but surprising responses
- **Original phrasing** - conversational and natural
- **Research-based** - studied effective Family Feud questions from corporate team building sources

## Question Format Variety

We now use **multiple question styles**:

### 🎯 Direct Questions
- "What do people forget to insure until disaster strikes?"
- "What's nearly impossible to keep clean if you're a painter?"
- "Where do accidents always seem to happen?"
- "Which color looks great in the store but terrible on the wall?"

### 📝 Fill in the Blank
- "Fill in the blank: When someone finds out they're not covered, they immediately ________."
- "Complete the lie: 'I'll start ________ tomorrow.'"
- "Complete the excuse: 'I'd save money, but...'"

### 💬 Statement Format
- "Families will fight over this after someone passes away:"
- "Something dangerous people do without thinking twice:"
- "You know it's bad for you, but you do it anyway:"
- "Seniors are always looking for their:"

### 🤔 Situational
- "At closing, homebuyers are shocked they have to pay for:"
- "Within a week of new carpet, someone will spill:"
- "During a home renovation, this always goes wrong:"
- "Before retiring, you wish someone had warned you about:"

## Key Improvements

### 1. **Original Question Formats (No More "Name..." Repetition)**
Every question now has a unique format:
- Direct questions: "What do...", "Where do...", "Which..."
- Fill-in-the-blank: "Complete the lie: 'I'll start ________ tomorrow.'"
- Statements: "Something dangerous people do without thinking twice:"
- Situational: "At closing, homebuyers are shocked they have to pay for:"

### 2. **Variety in Topics**
Each member now has questions that go beyond their job:
- Professional scenarios (relevant to their expertise)
- Common life situations (everyone can relate)
- Humorous but realistic situations

### 3. **Better Answer Structure**
- Top answer: 35-40 points (most obvious)
- Middle answers: 18-28 points (logical alternatives)
- Bottom answers: 2-10 points (funny but possible)
